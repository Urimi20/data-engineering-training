# Data Pipeline Thinking - Day 3

## Chosen business:

Online Store

## Source Data:

The source data comes from customer orders, product details, customer information, and payment records. This data is generated when customers browse products and place orders.

## Ingestion:

The data is collected from the online store system and loaded into a database where it can be processed and analyzed.

## Storage:

The data is stored in database tables such as orders, customers, and products. The database keeps the information organized and available for future analysis.

## Cleaning:

The data is cleaned by removing duplicate records, fixing missing customer information, correcting wrong prices, and standardizing order statuses.

## Transformation:

The data is transformed by calculating total order value using quantity \* price, analyzing sales performance, and creating useful summaries.

## Business Output:

The final output can be sales reports, revenue analysis, product performance reports, and dashboards for managers.

## Business questions we can answer:

1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. How many orders are paid, unpaid, or cancelled?
3. Which customers spend the most money?

## Possible data problems:

1. Missing or incorrect customer information.
2. Duplicate orders in the system.
3. Incorrect product prices or order statuses.
